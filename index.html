Meowlog

Your cat's voice, written down.

Meowlog listens for meows, catches each one automatically, and draws it as a melody. Tell it what a meow was about and it learns your cat's own way of asking for food, attention or the door.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email yajousaki@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Meowlog doesn't hear my cat. Hold the phone within about a metre of your cat and keep background noise (TV, music) low. Meows are detected on your phone, so it also works offline.

Are the translations real? They are for fun. The suggestions you see after labelling five meows come from your own cat's past meows, not from a universal cat language.
